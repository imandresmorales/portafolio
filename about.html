About me

I'm Andrés Morales, a Computer Science Engineer and Full Stack developer with experience in technologies such as React, Next.js, NestJS, Node.js, Flutter, Java and Python. I love building functional, accessible and well-structured web applications that combine a great user experience with a solid backend.

Since September 2025 I've been working as a Full Stack developer at a software company, building web and mobile apps in production: backends with authentication and real-time features, payment and e-commerce integrations, and performance improvements that cut an app's load time in half.

I've worked on institutional, educational and social impact projects, applying agile methodologies and good development practices. I enjoy learning on my own and exploring new technologies such as artificial intelligence and blockchain.

My focus is always on solving real problems through code, working with multidisciplinary teams and taking on new challenges with responsibility and commitment.
